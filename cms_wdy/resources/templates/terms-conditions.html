SOLE AGENCY / SOLE SELLING RIGHTS

If the Seller appoints the Agent to act as sole selling agent, they are entering into a legally binding contract giving the Agent the right to act as their only estate agent during the term of this agreement. You may be liable to pay the Agent the agreed fee and/or costs should a buyer be introduced by any other agent during the period of the sole agency agreement.


If the Seller appoints the Agent as sole agent the Seller will be liable to pay the Agent the agreed fee and costs if an unconditional contract is exchanged for the sale of the property/land to a purchaser:-


a) introduced by the Agent during the period of sole agency, or with whom the Agent had negotiations about the property/land during that period.

b) introduced by any other agent during the period of the sole agency.

c) who has made a private offer direct to the Seller.


MULTI/JOINT AGENCY

If the Seller appoints the Agent as multiple agent the Seller will be liable to pay the Agent the agreed fee and costs if an unconditional contract is exchanged at any time for the sale of the property/land to a purchaser:-


a) introduced by the Agent during the period of sole agency, or with whom the Agent had negotiations about the property/land during that period.


If the Seller terminates the agreement with the Agent, the Agent will be entitled to claim the agreed fee and costs for any person introduced to the property/land by the Agent during the period of the agreement, who proceeds with the purchase of the property/land through another agent, up to a period of six months from the termination date of the agreement.

IDENTITY OF BUYER

If the Seller accepts an offer for the property/land either privately or through any other agent, the Seller agrees to disclose the identity of the buyer to the Agent prior to exchange of contracts.

PAYMENTS AND COMMISSIONS

The agreed fee and costs will be payable if an unconditional contract is exchanged for the sale of the property/land. The Seller agrees to instruct their solicitor to pay the Agent's fee and costs out of the net proceeds of sale on legal completion of the sale, if not paid before. If any invoice remains unpaid after the date on which it is due, the cost incurred in recovering the debt may be added.

VAT

All charges made by the Agent are subject to VAT at the prevailing rate.

FAILED LEGAL COMPLETION

If contracts are exchanged and the buyer fails to complete the purchase, the Agent reserves the right to charge 50% of the agreed sale fee plus any outstanding marketing expenses, plus VAT.

The Agent accepts no liability for any consequential loss and the Seller agrees to indemnify the Agent in respect of any claims for such loss.

The Agent does not accept responsibility for the maintenance or repair of an unoccupied property unless we have an agreement in writing. It is the responsibility of the Seller to ensure that mains services are turned off, water and heating systems are professionally drained, and the insurers are notified.

LIABILITY

No liability is accepted for any indirect or consequential loss (such as loss of profits). These limitations do not apply to death, personal injury or fraud.

The Seller agrees not to bring any claim against any member, employee, or consultant of the Agent. These individuals do not have a personal duty of care. Any such claim must be brought against the Seller.
